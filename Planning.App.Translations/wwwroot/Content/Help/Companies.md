The **Companies** tab lists the railway companies that operate on your layout — the operators that
trains, train categories, driver duties and vehicles can belong to. It is the source for the company
choices shown wherever an operator is selected.

## What a company is

Each company has:

- a **name**, its full name as shown in lists and reports;
- a **signature**, the short abbreviation used to identify it (for example *DB* or *SJ*). The
  signature must be unique within the layout;
- a **country** it operates in. A company that operates in several countries is added once per
  country of operation, so each entry pairs a company with one country.

## How companies are used

A company on its own is just a catalogue entry. It becomes meaningful once it is assigned as the
operator of something — a **train**, a **train category**, a **driver duty**, or a **vehicle**. The
company's signature and country then appear wherever that object is shown.

## Managing companies

The list shows each company's name, signature and country.

- **Add new** creates a company; give it a name and a unique signature, and choose its country.
- **Add from catalogue** offers operating companies from the built-in catalogue whose country is in
  your layout, excluding those already added. Tick the ones you want and add them in one step. After
  adding a country on the **Countries** tab, reopen this to pick that country's companies.
- **Edit** changes a company's name, signature or country.
- **Delete** removes a company. It is blocked while the company is still referenced by any train,
  train category, driver duty or vehicle — hover the disabled **Delete** button to see what
  references it, then reassign or remove those first.

Companies are stored with the layout. Each company's country must be present in the layout's country
catalogue (**Countries** tab); the countries a company references are added there automatically.
