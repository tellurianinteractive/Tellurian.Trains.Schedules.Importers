The **Track occupancy** tab shows what stands on each track of each station over the day: one row for
every track, time along the axis.

### Reading the board

- A **coloured block** is a train, from its arrival to its departure, in the colour of its category.
  A train that only passes through is a thin mark.
- A **dashed, hatched block** is vehicles left standing on the track between their trains — before the
  first train of the day, between two trains, and after the last one. These are the same stays that
  are drawn as dashed lines in the **Graphical timetable**, and they are only shown when
  **Settings › Validation › Vehicles occupy the track while standing?** is ticked.
- When two or more blocks are on a track at the same time they are **stacked on top of each other**, each
  on its own line, so you can tell which is which. Stacking only means they overlap in time; whether
  they really are in each other's way depends on the sessions they run on.
- A block with a **red outline** is part of a conflict. Point at it to read what the conflict is.

Point at any block to see the train or vehicles, the track and the times.

### Moving a train to another track

Drag a train's block onto another track **of the same station** and drop it. The tracks it can be dropped
on are marked green while you drag. The train's call then uses the new track, and the conflicts are
checked again. Vehicles standing between trains cannot be dragged: they follow the trains.

### Choosing what to show

Tick the **stations** to show. Use **Zoom** to give the time axis more or less room; the board scrolls
sideways when it is wider than the window. A train running past midnight wraps around and continues from
the start of the axis.
