# Welcome to the Timetable Planner
This is where you plan a model railway operating session: the
track layout, the trains and their timings, and the schedules and duties that go with them.

## Getting started

- **Start from scratch** by by entering all data, or 
- **Partially import** resuable data and then refined and entering the reaming data, or
- **Open an existing plan** on the **Import** tab. You can open a previously saved planning
  document (`.json`) or import an XPLN spreadsheet (`.ods` / `.xlsx`).

Once a plan is loaded, the other tabs become useful: build trains on **Trains**, view the
**Graphic timetable**, and assign vehicles and duties on **Schedules**.

## The workspace tab

Each tab can be opened as a dockable view in the **Workspace**, so you can see several views side
by side — for example the graphic timetable next to the trains list. Drag a tab into the
workspace to dock it.

## Order of work
The tabs is ordered in the recommended steps to enter/import data:
- **Home** has an option to create a new *plan*.
- **Settings** is the place for values controlling visual appearence,
  timetable calculation and for importing and exporting data.
- **Regions** defines geographical directions for operation location outside the layout.
- **Operation Locations** are all stations and other types of places where train's passing times are recorded.
