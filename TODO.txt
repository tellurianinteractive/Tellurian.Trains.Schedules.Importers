Validation rules, the list below is not complete, byt should be compared with what's actually implemented:
1. Trains can only meet at stations and on stretches that has at least two tracks.
2. Trains cannot meet at the same track on a station. 
3. More that one train can end in the same track on a station.
4. The maximum number of trains in any direction on a track stretch is equal to the number of tracks.
5. Train call times must be ascending, and at an operation location arrival time must not be after departure time.
6. All train's parts must be part of a schedule. Parts can be in different schedules, the same part can be in different schedules for different operating sessions. No part can be assigned to a schedule with sessions overlapping the same part in another schedule. 
7. An all session/day schedule must start and end at the same station. If not, a schedule can be divided in two parts one starting at A and ends at B, the second starts at B and ends at A. Schedules can be split in any number of parts, but in the end the last part  must end where the first one starts. Schedule parts may overlap in time, and each part needs a starting traction unit.
8. A schdule must have a traction unit assigned to all of it's sessions. They can be traction units assigned for different operating sessions/days.

The effective operating sessions/days: trains may have session/days operating. When adding trains to a schedule, the operating sessions/days for that schedule becomes the session/days when all of these trains operate. This also implies that a train can't be added unless it operate at least one of the session/days the already added trains have in common. If the effective sessions/days is not all, the effective days of the scheudle should be inserted between the Delete button and the first train. On each train label, if the train does not operate all sessions/days, it should also display the sessions/days for that train.
